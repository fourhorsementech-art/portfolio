Who we are

About Four Horsemen Technologies

Four Horsemen Technologies (Pty) Ltd is a South African technology startup founded by graduates of Walter Sisulu University, united by a shared vision to deliver innovative, reliable, and affordable technology solutions that create meaningful impact.

We specialize in digital transformation services that empower individuals, small businesses, and organizations to thrive in an increasingly digital world. Our expertise spans software development, IT services and technical support, digital access solutions, and sustainable e-waste management.

Driven by technical excellence, innovation, and strong client relationships, we are committed to delivering solutions that solve real-world challenges while supporting long-term business growth. Our customer-focused approach ensures every solution is tailored to meet the unique needs of our clients.

At Four Horsemen Technologies, our mission is to strengthen our market presence, expand our portfolio of technology solutions, and become a trusted technology partner across South Africa — helping businesses and communities innovate, grow, and succeed in the digital era.
