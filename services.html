E-Waste Collection & Refurbishment

We collect the IT equipment your organisation has outgrown, give it a full technical second life, and put it into the hands of students and communities who need it most — closing the gap between corporate refresh cycles and digital inequality.

Device Collection

We collect desktops, laptops, servers, monitors, networking gear and accessories directly from your premises — no minimum volume required.

Secure Data Erasure

Every drive undergoes industry-recognised data destruction before anything else happens. Certificates of destruction provided on request.

Refurbishment

Faulty components replaced, storage upgraded to SSD where possible, licensed software installed, and every unit tested before release.

Student Matching

We match each device to what a student actually studies — a programmer needs different specs to a design or admin student — so nobody overpays or under-specs.
